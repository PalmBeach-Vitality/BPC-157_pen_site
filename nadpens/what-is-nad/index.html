What is NAD+?

NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a dinucleotide coenzyme. It is not a peptide. PubChem CID 5892 lists it as nadide, formula C21H27N7O14P2, molecular weight 663.4 g/mol, CAS 53-84-9.1
