02 · The compound

A dinucleotide, not a peptide chain.

NAD+ is nicotinamide joined to adenine through two ribose sugars and a pyrophosphate bridge. The seller lists the vial as an acetate salt, already dissolved. Laboratories use NAD+ as a redox cofactor stock. This page does not turn that into a human result, and it does not give a dose.

07 · Questions

Common questions.

Is this NAD+ pen an FDA-approved drug?

No. It is sold for laboratory research use only. The research-use label is not an approval.

Does the pen or the vial need reconstitution?

No. The seller lists both as ready-to-use aqueous solution.

How should they be stored?

Refrigerate at 2–8 °C (36–46 °F), protect from light, and do not freeze. The seller’s practical shelf life is 4–6 weeks for a pen and 16–20 weeks for a vial.

Why is there no dosing on this page?

The products are sold for laboratory research only. Dosing would be guidance this site does not give.
