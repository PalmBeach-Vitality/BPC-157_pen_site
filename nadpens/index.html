NAD+, in pen and vial.

NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a dinucleotide coenzyme. It is not a peptide. Palm Beach Vitality sells it as three pre-filled 3 mL pens and two 10 mL vials. The seller says each of those five ships already in aqueous solution, for laboratory research use only.

06 What is not claimed

No borrowed status.

This page does not state an FDA approval status for NAD+, and it does not state a WADA status. Those lines are absent because a primary source for them is not cited here. BPC-157’s S0 status is a different compound and stays on bpc157pens.com.

Is NAD+ a 15-residue peptide?

No. PubChem CID 5892 gives the formula C21H27N7O14P2 and the mass 663.4 g/mol, and its synonyms include nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide.1

Do the pens and vials ship already in solution?

Yes, for the five products listed above. The seller says they are already in aqueous solution and that no reconstitution is required. Freeze-dried NAD+ is the bulk order the vial pages tell a buyer to ask about.2

What do the 300 clicks mean?

Each pen page lists 300 clicks and 0.01 mL per click. That is a description of the cartridge. It is not an instruction for use, and this site does not say how many clicks to take.2

Why is there no dosing?

The products are sold for laboratory research use only. Dosing would be medical advice. This site does not give it.
