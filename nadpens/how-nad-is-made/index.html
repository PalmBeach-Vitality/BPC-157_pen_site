01 The products

Already in solution.

A route is printed here only when a primary source describes it. The PubChem record cited on this site is an identity record. It is not used here as a bench method.

Palm Beach Vitality says the three pens and two liquid vials need no reconstitution.2 Freeze-dried NAD+ is a separate bulk order. The vial pages say to contact the seller, and that a minimum order applies. Contact Palm Beach Vitality.

The homepage film names nicotinamide and adenine. The caption calls that an artistic picture, not a scale model, and not a picture of the pen. Naming the two parts is not a recipe for joining them.

02 What is withheld

No steps. No clicks.

Each pen page lists 300 clicks and 0.01 mL per click. Those figures describe the cartridge. They are not instructions for use, and this page does not say how many clicks to take.2
