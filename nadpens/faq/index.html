Questions

Is NAD+ a 15-residue peptide?

No. PubChem CID 5892 gives the formula C21H27N7O14P2 and the mass 663.4 g/mol, and its synonyms include nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide.1

Do the pens and vials ship already in solution?

Yes, for the five products on the homepage. The seller says they are already in aqueous solution and that no reconstitution is required. Freeze-dried NAD+ is the bulk order the vial pages tell a buyer to ask about.2

What do the 300 clicks mean?

Each pen page lists 300 clicks and 0.01 mL per click. That is a description of the cartridge. It is not an instruction for use, and this site does not say how many clicks to take.2

Why is there no dosing?

The products are sold for laboratory research use only. Dosing would be medical advice. This site does not give it.

Who owns this site?

nadpens.com is owned by the same company as Palm Beach Vitality. The company benefits when a reader buys through the links. Prices were checked November 25, 2026.

Is there a certificate on this site?

No. The seller says lot-specific COAs are available on request. This site does not publish one.2

Does this site state an FDA or WADA status?

No. A primary source for either status is not cited here. BPC-157’s S0 status stays on bpc157pens.com.
