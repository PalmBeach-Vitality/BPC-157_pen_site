How to store BPC-157.

Keep BPC-157 pens and liquid vials refrigerated at 2–8 °C (36–46 °F), protected from light, and never frozen. The seller lists a practical shelf life of 4–6 weeks for pens and 16–20 weeks for liquid vials.1

The right temperature

Store both liquid formats in a refrigerator between 2 and 8 °C. Do not freeze them, and avoid long periods at room temperature.1

Listed shelf life

Liquid vials have about three times the listed refrigerated shelf life of pens: 16–20 weeks versus 4–6 weeks.1

Storage checklist

  • Temperature: 2–8 °C (36–46 °F). Avoid long periods at room temperature.
  • Freezing: do not freeze either liquid format.
  • Light and moisture: keep in the original packaging, away from light.
  • Discard: after the seller’s shelf-life window, or sooner if the solution turns cloudy, discolored or shows particles.
  • Records: note the lot number and the date the pen or vial was first opened.
