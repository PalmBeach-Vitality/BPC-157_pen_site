About this site

About bpc157pens.com

bpc157pens.com is a research education site about BPC-157 pens and vials, written by the bpc157pens.com Editorial Team. It is owned by the same company as Palm Beach Vitality.

How we write this site

  • Every factual claim links to a published study, an official regulator page, or the seller’s product specifications.
  • We separate cell and animal findings from human evidence and say plainly when evidence is weak.
  • Study authors are credited for their published research. None of them wrote, reviewed or endorsed this site.
  • We never give dosing, usage or medical guidance.
  • We review every page at least every 90 days and whenever FDA or WADA changes BPC-157’s status. Each page shows its last review date.

Ownership and disclosure

Affiliated store

bpc157pens.com and Palm Beach Vitality are owned by the same company. Product links on this site go to Palm Beach Vitality, and the company benefits when you buy there.

Products linked here are sold for laboratory research use only, ship to U.S. addresses only, and are not for human consumption. This site is for readers 18 and older.

About the images

Lab scenes on this site are illustrative and do not show a specific manufacturer’s facility. Molecule visuals are artistic, not to scale. Product photos come from Palm Beach Vitality.
