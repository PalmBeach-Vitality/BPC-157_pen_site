Lab note · November 2, 2026

The advisory vote is not a final rule

On July 23, 2026, an FDA advisory committee voted 8–6, with one abstention, to recommend adding BPC-157 to the 503A bulk drug substances list. FDA has not finalized any change. BPC-157 is still not approved for human or veterinary use.

By the bpc157pens.com Editorial Team · November 2, 2026

What the vote was

The Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee gives FDA advice. A vote is not a regulation. In 2023 FDA had listed BPC-157 among bulk substances that may present significant safety risks for compounding. The July 2026 vote recommended going the other way. Until FDA finishes its own process, the 2023 listing is the agency position this site can point to.

“Research use only” on a label is not that process. It does not make BPC-157 a drug, a dietary supplement, or a product for human use. This note is general information, not legal advice.

Sport is a separate list

The World Anti-Doping Agency prohibits BPC-157 at all times under S0, non-approved substances. The U.S. Department of Defense also lists it as prohibited for service members. An advisory vote about compounding does not remove either of those.
