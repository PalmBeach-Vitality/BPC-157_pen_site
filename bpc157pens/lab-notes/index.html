November 16, 2026

What to read on a BPC-157 certificate

A useful certificate names the compound, matches the lot on the label, and reports purity and identity. It is not an approval.

November 9, 2026

Pens and vials that arrive already in solution

The standard BPC-157 pen and the liquid vial ship already in solution. Freeze-dried peptide is the format that has to be reconstituted, and it is a bulk order.

November 2, 2026

The advisory vote is not a final rule

An FDA advisory committee voted in July 2026 to recommend a compounding change for BPC-157. FDA has not finalized that change.

October 2026

October 26, 2026

Two pen strengths, one cartridge size

Both BPC-157 pens use a 3 mL cartridge. The 20 mg pen is about twice the concentration of the 10 mg pen. That is a label fact, not a dose.

What the human-evidence review counted

A 2026 systematic review found three small human studies, 30 participants in total, and rated the certainty very low. Most published BPC-157 work is cell and animal research.

October 5, 2026

Fifteen residues, written out

BPC-157 is the synthetic peptide GEPPPGKPADDAGLV, formula C62H98N16O22, about 1419.5 g/mol. The pictures on this site are artistic, not a scale model.
