Pens and vials that arrive already in solution

Every standard BPC-157 pen and liquid vial Palm Beach Vitality sells ships already in solution. There is no mixing step for those two formats. Freeze-dried BPC-157 is the form that has to be reconstituted, and the seller offers it for bulk orders only.

By the bpc157pens.com Editorial Team · November 9, 2026

Two ready formats

The pen is a sealed 3 mL cartridge with a dial. The liquid vial holds 10 mL and is drawn with separate lab tools. Both are filled before they ship. The distinction is the container and the listed shelf life, not a mixing recipe.

The seller lists a practical refrigerated shelf life of 4 to 6 weeks for pens and 16 to 20 weeks for liquid vials, at 2 to 8 °C, protected from light, and not frozen. Those figures are the seller’s storage statement. They are not a stability study published by this site.

The format this note does not teach

Lyophilized peptide is a dry solid. Putting it into solution is reconstitution. This site does not give that procedure. The only place Palm Beach Vitality lists freeze-dried BPC-157 is as a bulk order, separate from the pen and the liquid vial.

A research-use label still means laboratory material. It does not turn either format into an approved drug.
