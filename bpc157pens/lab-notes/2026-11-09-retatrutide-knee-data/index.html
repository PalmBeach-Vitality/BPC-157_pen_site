Retatrutide knee data and a semaglutide heart study

MUST-KNOW

November 2 to November 9 brought two positive trial readouts on incretin peptides. Both are prescription-drug studies. They do not describe research-use-only material.

1) Retatrutide in painful knee osteoarthritis and obesity (New England Journal of Medicine, November 8, 2026). TRIUMPH-4 and TRIUMPH-1-OA assigned adults with painful knee osteoarthritis and obesity to once-weekly retatrutide or placebo. Retatrutide is an investigational triple agonist of GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors. In TRIUMPH-4, at 68 weeks, the hybrid-treatment differences in WOMAC pain versus placebo were −1.83 points at 9 mg and −1.61 points at 12 mg. Mean body weight changed by −20.0% at 9 mg and −23.7% at 12 mg, versus −4.6% on placebo. TRIUMPH-1-OA also showed greater pain reduction at 80 weeks. Serious adverse events were more common with retatrutide than with placebo, and the most common adverse events were gastrointestinal. Retatrutide remains investigational. These results do not support use of a research peptide.

2) Semaglutide in obesity-related HFpEF (Nature Medicine, November 8, 2026). In CAMEO-SEMA, 81 adults with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction and obesity were assigned to semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly or placebo for 52 weeks. Exercise pulmonary artery wedge pressure fell by an estimated 10.6 mmHg versus placebo. Hemodynamic remission, normal filling pressure at rest and during exercise, occurred in 53.7% on semaglutide versus 7.4% on placebo. A cardiac MRI substudy the same day, in 49 of those participants, found better left-atrial reservoir strain, left-ventricular filling, left-ventricular mass and geometry, and right-ventricular function. Semaglutide is an FDA-approved prescription drug. These findings do not apply to unapproved research peptides.

ALSO ON THE RADAR

BPC-157 production and a peptide wound gel

BPC-157 fusion proteins in a rat ulcer model (Applied Biochemistry and Biotechnology, November 2026 issue; online August 29). Zhang and colleagues integrated GGT-BPC157 and mScarlet-BPC157 into Bacillus licheniformis. Under the fermentation conditions they tested, reporter signals rose about threefold versus the basal medium. In an ethanol-induced acute gastric ulcer model in rats, ulcer inhibition was 75.3% to 82.9% in the protective regimen and 80.4% to 84.6% in the therapeutic regimen. Histology showed less mucosal inflammation and more glandular repair, and TNF-alpha, IL-1 beta, and IL-6 were lower in gastric tissue. This is a rat study of fusion proteins. It is not a human result and it is not a production recipe.

A peptide nanozyme gel in a diabetic infected-wound model (ACS Applied Materials and Interfaces, November 2, 2026). Shen and colleagues describe a designed peptide, C7-RD(S)HHEEH, coordinated with copper, that mimics several enzyme activities. In a sprayable gelatin hydrogel, the gel accelerated wound closure, angiogenesis, and collagen deposition in a diabetic bacteria-infected wound model and was described as biocompatible. This is preclinical materials work.
