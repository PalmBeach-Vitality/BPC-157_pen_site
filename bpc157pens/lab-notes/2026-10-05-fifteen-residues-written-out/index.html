Fifteen residues, written out

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide of 15 amino acids: GEPPPGKPADDAGLV. The molecular formula is C62H98N16O22. The molecular weight is 1419.5 g/mol. The CAS registry number is 137525-51-0.

By the bpc157pens.com Editorial Team · October 5, 2026

The chain

Fifteen residues means fourteen peptide bonds. The sequence is written from the N-terminus. Glycine, glutamic acid, then four prolines in the first six positions, and valine at the end. The peptide is modeled on a fragment of a protein found in human gastric juice. A research group at the University of Zagreb published the early description. That citation credits their paper. It is not an endorsement of this site or of any product.

Solid-phase synthesis builds the chain from the other direction: valine is anchored on the resin and glycine is added last. The finished chain is cut off, purified by HPLC, and checked by mass spectrometry before it is filled. That process is the subject of the manufacturing guide, not a bench protocol a reader can run from this note.

Pictures are not measurements

The chain film and the residue row on this site are artistic. Each sphere stands for one amino acid. They are not to scale, and they are not a photograph of a lot. Identity still comes from the analytical record: mass near 1419.5 g/mol and an HPLC purity result on a certificate that names the lot.
