Two pen strengths, one cartridge size

Both BPC-157 pens use the same 3 mL cartridge. The 20 mg pen holds twice the peptide, so the concentration is about 6.67 mg/mL against about 3.33 mg/mL for the 10 mg pen. The liquid vials are a different container: 10 mL at 1 mg/mL (10 mg) or 2 mg/mL (20 mg). These are labeled amounts.

By the bpc157pens.com Editorial Team · October 26, 2026

What the numbers are

Divide the labeled milligrams by the labeled milliliters and you get the concentration printed in the comparison on this site. Nothing in that arithmetic says how a laboratory should meter the solution. A dial on the pen measures volume. This note does not convert volume into a use instruction.

The vial is not a larger pen. It is 10 mL of solution at a lower concentration than the matching pen, with a longer listed shelf life. Choosing between them is a format choice: cartridge and dial, or a vial drawn with separate tools.

Where the numbers come from

The amounts are Palm Beach Vitality’s product specifications, checked for this site. They can change. The comparison page is the place that table lives, and it should be reread if the seller edits a listing.
