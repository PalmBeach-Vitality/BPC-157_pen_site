What is KLOW?

KLOW is Palm Beach Vitality’s name for a research solution of four peptides: KPV, BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu. It is not one 15-residue chain. Read more →

Is KLOW an FDA-approved drug?

No. KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug. It is labeled for laboratory research use only. BPC-157, one component, has its own FDA record on bpc157pens.com. That record is not the history of the blend. Read more →

Is the name KLOW on the WADA list?

This site does not say that. BPC-157 is prohibited in sport under WADA S0. That status belongs to the component and is explained on the BPC-157 legal page. No status is invented here for the name KLOW.
