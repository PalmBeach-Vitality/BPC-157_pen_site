What is KLOW?

KLOW is the name Palm Beach Vitality uses for a solution of four peptides: KPV, BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu. Both the pen and the vial are labeled 10 mg, 10 mg, 10 mg, and 50 mg. The blend is not one 15-residue chain, and KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug.1,2

The four components

Each name is a different molecule. They share a vial or a pen. They do not share one sequence.

What KLOW is not

  • Not one chain. Four peptides share the solution. BPC-157’s 15 residues are one of those peptides.
  • Not an approved drug. KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug. It is labeled for laboratory research use only.1
  • Not a studied combination. There is no published study of this four-peptide pen or vial as one product. The research page says what the component papers actually are.
  • Not a use protocol. This site does not give dosing, click-count instructions for use, or a reconstitution guide. Both catalog formats are already in aqueous solution.

Where the papers fit

The seller’s KLOW reading list is five papers. Each one is about one component, or about the longer thymosin beta-4 molecule. None of them is a study of this pen or vial.
