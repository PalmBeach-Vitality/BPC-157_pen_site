The blend

There is no FDA approval to cite for the KLOW pen or the KLOW vial. The seller’s pages describe a research blend, not an approved drug.1,2

This site does not invent a compounding history, an advisory-committee vote, or a military listing for the name KLOW. Those would be a different substance’s story, told as if it were this one.

One component’s record

BPC-157, one of the four components, has its own FDA record. That record is not the history of the blend.

BPC-157 only

The summary of that component’s FDA record, including the 2023 listing and the July 2026 advisory vote, is on bpc157pens.com/bpc-157-legal-status/. Read it as a page about BPC-157. Do not read it as a page about KLOW.

Sport

This site does not say the name KLOW is on the WADA list. No status is invented here for the blend name.

BPC-157 · WADA S0

The component, not the blend name

BPC-157 is prohibited in sport under WADA S0, non-approved substances. The explanation of that prohibition is on the BPC-157 legal page. It applies to that component. It is not a listing this site is claiming for the word KLOW.

What “research use only” means

“Research use only” is a label for material intended for laboratory work. It is not an FDA approval. It does not make KLOW a drug, a dietary supplement, or a product for human or veterinary use.

This page is general information for U.S. researchers, not legal advice. It is not a dosing, click-count, or reconstitution guide.
