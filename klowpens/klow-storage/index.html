How to store KLOW.

Refrigerate the pen and the liquid vial at 2–8 °C (36–46 °F). Do not freeze them. Protect them from light. The seller’s practical shelf life is 4–6 weeks for the pen and 16–20 weeks for the vial.1,2

The right temperature

The pen page states 2–8 °C (36–46 °F). The vial page states 36–46 °F and the same do-not-freeze rule. Both say to avoid a long time at room temperature.1,2

Listed shelf life

The seller’s practical refrigerated shelf life for the vial is 16–20 weeks. For the pen it is 4–6 weeks. The pen page also says to discard after 6 weeks. The vial page says to discard after 20 weeks. Either way, discard sooner if the liquid turns cloudy, changes color, or shows particles.1,2

Storage checklist

  • Temperature: 2–8 °C (36–46 °F). Avoid a long time at room temperature.
  • Freezing: do not freeze the pen or the liquid vial.
  • Light: protect from light. Keep the original packaging.
  • Discard: after the seller’s window, or sooner if the solution is cloudy, discolored, or shows particles.
  • Records: keep the lot number with the pen or vial.
