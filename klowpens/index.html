Research guide · Updated November 18, 2026

KLOW, in pen and vial.

KLOW is a research blend of four peptides in one solution: KPV, BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu. It is not one 15-residue chain. KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug. There is no published study of this pen or vial as one product. Palm Beach Vitality sells it for laboratory research use only, to U.S. researchers, as a 3 mL pen and a 10 mL vial.

§6 U.S. status

Not an approved drug.

KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug. This page does not give the blend a regulatory history it does not have.

  1. The blend

    Palm Beach Vitality sells the pen and the vial labeled for laboratory research use only. That label is not an FDA approval.

  2. One component

    BPC-157, one of the four, has its own FDA record. It is summarized on bpc157pens.com. That record is not the history of KLOW.

  3. Sport

    This site does not say the name KLOW is on the WADA list. BPC-157 is prohibited in sport under WADA S0. That component note is on the same BPC-157 page.

§7 FAQ

Common questions.

Short answers. The full list is on the FAQ page. None of them is a dosing, click-count, or reconstitution guide.

All questions
Is KLOW an FDA-approved drug?

No. KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug. It is sold for laboratory research use only.

Does the KLOW pen or vial need reconstitution?

No. Both ship already in aqueous solution. Freeze-dried KLOW is a bulk order. The seller asks you to make contact, and a minimum applies. This site does not give a reconstitution guide.

How should the pen and vial be stored?

Refrigerate at 2–8 °C (36–46 °F), protect from light, and do not freeze. The seller’s practical shelf life is 4–6 weeks for the pen and 16–20 weeks for the vial.

Why doesn’t this site give click counts for use?

The pen’s 300 clicks and 0.01 mL per click are format specifications, the way the seller lists them. Turning those numbers into instructions for use would be guidance this site does not give.
