KLOW pen vs vial.

The pen is a sealed 3 mL cartridge with a built-in dial. The vial holds 10 mL and is drawn with separate lab tools. Both ship already in aqueous solution, with the same labeled amounts: 10 mg KPV, 10 mg BPC-157, 10 mg TB-500, and 50 mg GHK-Cu. The pen is $232. The vial is $222. Freeze-dried KLOW is a bulk order.1,2

Anatomy of each format

The pen keeps the blend in a cartridge and moves volume with its dial. The vial is a glass container with a stopper under a crimped seal and a blue cap. The drawing is a diagram. The photographs below it are the seller’s.

The pen’s format specs

Palm Beach Vitality lists the pen as a 3 mL pre-filled cartridge with 300 clicks, 0.01 mL per click, and a dial marked 1–60.1

Three hundred clicks at 0.01 mL per click is 3 mL, which is the fill volume. The dial range is part of that same format description. This page does not turn the click count, the volume per click, or the dial marks into instructions for use.

The seller describes the liquid as an aqueous solution in acetate salt form. No reconstitution is listed for the pen.

The vial, and bulk powder

The liquid vial is 10 mL and is already in solution. The seller’s practical refrigerated shelf life for it is 16–20 weeks, longer than the pen’s 4–6 weeks.2

Freeze-dried KLOW is not one of the two liquid listings. The vial page says to contact the seller, and that a minimum applies. Contact Palm Beach Vitality. This site does not give a reconstitution guide.

The two products

Both are sold for laboratory research use only. Lot-specific certificates are available on request. The pen certificate this site shows is on the testing page.
