Enforcement and demand

INDUSTRY UPDATE

Sep 14–21 centered on enforcement and demand signals — not new peer-reviewed papers on the catalog peptides we track.

Must-know

1) Ohio Board of Pharmacy (Sep 14, WSJ) — 1,102 proactive inspections (Mar 2025–Aug 2026); 54 emergency shutdowns. Illicit retatrutide found (one clinic >400 vials). “Research-use” labels in clinical settings are being treated as unapproved-drug evidence. Retatrutide remains off-limits for compounding.

2) BPC-157 clinical notes (Sep 15, Reuters) — nference found 1,039 patients with clinician-documented use across >15M records; new users rose 33-fold (2020–2026). “Wolverine stack” (BPC-157 + TB-500) still circulates. Both remain RUO — no FDA-approved indication or finalized 503A bulk listing.

3) Partnership for Safe Medicines (Sep 15) — “Compounded GLP-1 Crisis” cites >1,700 adverse-event reports and marketing that implies equivalence to FDA-approved products. Pressure on residual 503A/503B activity and adjacent peptides (retatrutide, cagrilintide).
