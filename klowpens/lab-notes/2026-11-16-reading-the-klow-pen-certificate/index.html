Reading the KLOW pen certificate

The KLOW pen certificate on this site is an Accumark Labs report for one lot, not a description of every pen. Sample 8V4W-UP8E, lot KL102126, was received October 22, 2026, and the report was published October 26, 2026.

By the klowpens.com Editorial Team · November 16, 2026

The numbers on this lot

The certificate’s declared size is 80.0 mg. The measured total is 77.046 mg. Blend purity is reported as 99.78%. Page 2 separates the four analytes: KPV 11.039 mg at 99.092% purity, GHK-Cu 45.219 mg at 99.884%, BPC-157 9.688 mg at 99.881%, and TB500 (17–23 fragment) 11.1 mg at 99.986%.

The product label is a different sentence: 10 mg KPV, 10 mg BPC-157, 10 mg TB-500, and 50 mg GHK-Cu. The certificate does not rewrite that label. It says what the lab measured in lot KL102126. The next lot needs its own page.

What the report is not

A purity percentage is not an FDA approval, and it is not a study of the blend in a person or an animal. The name on the thymosin-related line is the fragment the lab was asked to measure. It is not a claim that the full thymosin beta-4 protein was in the pen.
