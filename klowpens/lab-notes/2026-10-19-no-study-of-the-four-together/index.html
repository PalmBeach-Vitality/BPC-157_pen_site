Lab note · October 19, 2026

No study of the four peptides together

There is no published study of this four-peptide pen or vial as one product. The seller’s reading list is five papers. Each one is about a component, or about a related peptide. None of them is a test of KLOW. The authors did not write, review, or endorse this site.

By the klowpens.com Editorial Team · October 19, 2026

The five papers, and the limit on each

Dalmasso and colleagues, 2008 (PMID 18061177), and Getting, Schiöth, and Perretti, 2003 (PMID 12750433), are KPV papers. Seiwerth and colleagues, 2021 (PMID 34267654), is a BPC-157 paper. Pickart, 2008 (PMID 18644225), is about GHK and GHK-Cu. Goldstein and colleagues, 2012 (PMID 22074294), is about thymosin β4, not the 17–23 fragment named on the pen certificate, and not the blend.

A result in one of those papers stays in that paper. It does not become evidence that the four peptides, combined at the labeled amounts, did the same thing. This site’s research page says that in longer form.

Why the list is still on the site

The list is there so a reader can see what the seller pointed to, and so this site can say plainly what those papers are not. Citing an author is credit for the paper. It is not a quote, and it is not a partnership.
