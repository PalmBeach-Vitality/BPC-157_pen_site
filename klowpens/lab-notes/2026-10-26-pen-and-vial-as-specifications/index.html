Pen and vial, as specifications

The KLOW pen and the KLOW vial carry the same labeled amounts: 10 mg KPV, 10 mg BPC-157, 10 mg TB-500, and 50 mg GHK-Cu. The pen puts them in 3 mL. The vial puts them in 10 mL. The difference is concentration and container, not a different recipe.

By the klowpens.com Editorial Team · October 26, 2026

The two containers

In the pen, those amounts are about 3.33 mg/mL for KPV, BPC-157, and TB-500, and about 16.67 mg/mL for GHK-Cu. The seller lists 300 clicks, 0.01 mL per click, and a dial marked 1–60. Those are descriptions of the device. They are not instructions to use it.

In the vial the same amounts are 1 mg/mL, 1 mg/mL, 1 mg/mL, and 5 mg/mL in 10 mL. The seller’s prices, checked November 18, 2026, were $232 for the pen and $222 for the vial. Prices move. The product pages are the record.

Shelf life stays with the seller

Practical shelf life, as listed: 4 to 6 weeks for the pen and 16 to 20 weeks for the liquid vial, refrigerated at 2 to 8 °C, not frozen, protected from light. This note does not add a stability experiment of its own.
