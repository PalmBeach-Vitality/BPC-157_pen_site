Four peptides in one solution

KLOW is four peptides in one aqueous solution: KPV, BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu. It is not one 15-residue chain. The pen and the ready-to-use vial are filled before they ship.

By the klowpens.com Editorial Team · November 9, 2026

What is in the container

KPV is the tripeptide Lys-Pro-Val. BPC-157 is the synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide. TB-500 on this label is the seller’s synthetic actin-binding fragment of thymosin beta-4. GHK-Cu is the copper complex of glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine. Putting them in one bottle does not make them one molecule, and it does not make a paper about one of them a paper about the blend.

The pen holds the labeled amounts in 3 mL. The vial holds the same labeled amounts in 10 mL. Both are already in solution. Freeze-dried KLOW is a separate bulk order. This site does not give a reconstitution guide for it.

Storage, as the seller states it

Refrigerate at 2 to 8 °C. Do not freeze. Protect from light. The seller’s practical shelf life is 4 to 6 weeks for the pen and 16 to 20 weeks for the liquid vial. Those are seller specifications.
