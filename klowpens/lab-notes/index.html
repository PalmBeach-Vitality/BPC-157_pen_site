November 16, 2026

Reading the KLOW pen certificate

The Accumark certificate for one pen lot names four analytes, a measured total, and a blend purity. Those figures are that lot, not a new label.

November 9, 2026

Four peptides in one solution

KLOW is KPV, BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu in one aqueous solution. It is not a single 15-residue chain. The pen and the vial ship already dissolved.

November 2, 2026

The blend is not an approved drug

KLOW as sold is not an FDA-approved drug. BPC-157, one component, has its own regulatory record. That record is not a status for the name KLOW.

Pen and vial, as specifications

The pen is 3 mL and the vial is 10 mL, with the same labeled peptide amounts. Click marks and milliliters are format facts, not instructions for use.

October 19, 2026

No study of the four peptides together

There is no published study of this four-peptide pen or vial as one product. The seller’s reading list is five papers about components, and the authors did not endorse this site.

October 5, 2026

The label and the certificate are different sentences

The label says 10, 10, 10, and 50 mg. One pen certificate measured 77.046 mg in total, with each analyte near, not identical to, the label. Both can be true.
